# Butler shooting: preliminary research notes (round 1)

Compiled 2026-10-12 for Sam's "how likely was the ear" project. Every figure has a source link next to it. Labels: **[S]** a source states it. **[I]** our inference or calculation. **[U]** low-reliability source (blog, forum, partisan outlet), kept because nothing better exists.

---

## 0. The short version

- **Geometry.** Crooks fired from the south end of the AGR International roof. He was about **130–150 m (140–165 yd)** north-northeast of the podium. Ground across the site is flat to within a few feet. The shot was nearly level, dropping about 1°. The AGR building sat at roughly Trump's **3 o'clock**, to his right. He had just turned his head right toward the immigration chart.
- **Shots.** Crooks fired **8 rounds in about 5.1 s**, in two bursts: 3 aimed shots over 1.55 s, a 2.8 s pause, then 5 fast shots about 0.2 s apart. A local officer's shot at about 5.9 s hit Crooks's rifle stock and stopped him. A Secret Service counter-sniper killed him at about 15.9 s, from about 180–200 yd.
- **Where the rounds went.**
  - Shot 1 grazed the top of Trump's right ear and continued, undeflected, into the bleachers behind him.
  - Shot 2 probably hit David Dutch, behind Trump on a similar line.
  - Shot 3 probably hit a crane or heavy equipment.
  - The second burst probably killed Corey Comperatore and wounded James Copenhaver.
  - About 2–3 rounds have **no published impact point**.
  - The FBI says every round is "accounted for" but has never published a per-round map.
- **The distribution problem.** Only **shot 1** was fired at an unaware, stationary Trump. Shots 2–3 were fired while he was going down. Shots 4–8 came after he was covered. **Crooks's real shots will not give us a usable "group".** At best they are a sanity check. The accuracy model will have to come mostly from benchmarks for comparable shooters, which do exist.
- **Rifle.** A DPMS AR-15 (5.56/.223, 16" barrel) with a **Holosun AEMS 1× red dot**. That is a non-magnifying dot sight, so the early "iron sights" reports were wrong. Crooks had 43 logged range sessions in 11 months, mostly rifle practice, the last one the day before.
- **Published odds.** We found **no published probability estimate** of an ear hit versus a lethal hit. Nobody has done this analysis yet.
- **Back-of-envelope.** Seen from the shooter's line, the "ear only, survivable" zone is a thin rim about 0.4–0.7 in wide on the edge of the head. That is roughly **2% of the head-plus-neck target area**. This is a rough illustration, not a result.

- **Building.** The AGR International (American Glass Research) complex at 615 Whitestown Rd, a set of interconnected corrugated-metal buildings outside the Secret Service perimeter. - **Our own calculation [I].** We used the FEMA roof footprint and Wikipedia's fairground point (40.85703, −79.971) as a stand-in for the stage.
  - Roof centroid: 136 m (445 ft) at bearing 014°.
  - Nearest and farthest roof corners: 118–144 m.
  - The shot travelled roughly south-southwest (185–205°).

### 1.3 Elevation
- **Ground.** USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM (EPQS API): the AGR block is at 1,335.5 ft, the stage area 1,336.2 ft, and both barns about 1,336 ft. **The site is flat to within about 3 ft.** [S data]
- **Line of fire [I].**
  - Muzzle about 14–17 ft above ground.
  - Trump's ear about 10 ft above ground: a stage of about 4 ft (amateur estimate [U]) plus his 6'3".
  - That gives a downward angle of about **0.6–1.0°**, so the shot was effectively level.
  - Oliver Alexander independently got −0.64°. [S] [thread](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1814300337299165562.html)

### 1.4 Trump's orientation and head turn
- **Where AGR sat relative to Trump.** USSS documents put the AGR buildings at "approximately three o'clock" from the stage. [S] [HSGAC final, July 2025, fn. clviii](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/USSS-Chairman-Report-Final-July-20254.pdf)
  - Counter-snipers were "behind Trump's left shoulder" and "over Trump's right shoulder." [S] [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/texas/news/trump-rally-shooting-tree-snipers-analysis-video-satellite-imagery-cbs-news)
  - [I] Trump faced roughly W–WNW, and the shooter was about 90° to his right.
- **The head turn.** Right before shot 1 he turned his head right and up toward the chart on the screen. [S] Wikipedia; [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/donald-trump-rally-shooting-head-tilt-1925046)
  - Trump: "If I only half-turn, it hits the back of the brain."
  - **No measured rotation angle exists.** dschmitt estimated about 23° of head and torso tilt. [U] [dschmitt](https://dschmitt.substack.com/p/supposed-image-of-a-bullet-passing)
- **Open check [I].** The NYT says the bullet continued into bleachers "to his left," yet the shooter was to his right. We need to reconcile the stage layout and his facing direction before we model the head pose. This is likely a matter of whose left (the camera's or his), but we haven't verified it.

### What this means for "the distribution" [I]
- **Shot 1** is the only round fired at a stationary, unaware Trump.
- **Shots 2–3** were aimed at roughly the same spot as Trump dropped. Shot 2 hit Dutch about 10'6" up, almost the same height as shot 1's bleacher strike, which suggests a tight vertical pair.
- **Shots 4–8** were fast fire into the crowd and stage area after Trump was covered.
- So the real-world "group" on Trump is 1–3 shots with partial impact data. That is enough to sanity-check an accuracy model, but not to fit one.

## 5. Rifle, ammo, and shooter skill

### Rifle
- **DPMS Panther Arms AR-15 (A-15/DR-15), 5.56×45/.223, 16" barrel.**
  - Aero Precision rail, **Holosun AEMS 1× red dot** (2 MOA dot plus a 65 MOA circle), Magpul collapsible stock, possibly 45° offset iron sights. [S] [FBI evidence photos](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/butler-investigation-photos); [AEMS spec](https://www.midwestgunworks.com/page/mgwi/prod/aems-221301)
  - Barrel twist: unknown.
  - Generic rifle accuracy: about 2–3 MOA. [S/U]
- **Ammunition.** 50 rounds of 5.56 bought the same day. Brand and bullet weight have not been published. [S] House TF final report p. 24; [ABC](https://abc7ny.com/post/what-thomas-matthew-crooks-did-hours-leading-up-to-attempted-assassination/15067382/)
- **His own practice.** FBI files released Sept 2026 quote his father saying Crooks "wanted to see how far he could shoot with rudimentary sights." [S] [WJLA](https://wjla.com/news/nation-world/fbi-releases-new-details-about-thomas-crooks-gun-obsession-pseudonym-bob-dole-butler-pennsylvania-todd-blanche-)
- **He used a rangefinder.** [S] House TF

### Ballistics at ~135–150 yd [I]
Point-mass G1 calculation, ±5%:

| Load (16") | MV fps | V @130 yd | Time of flight @130 yd | POI @130 yd, 50 yd zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 55 gr M193 | 3,050 | ~2,680 | 0.136 s | +2.0 in |
| 62 gr M855 | 2,950 | ~2,660 | 0.139 s | +1.9 in |
| 77 gr OTM | 2,650 | ~2,425 | 0.154 s | +1.4 in |

- Zero choice moves the point of impact by at most about 2 in, so drop is a minor error term.
- Wind was about 4–7 mph, which gives about 2 in of drift. [S] [McBeth](https://ryanmcbeth.substack.com/p/initial-thoughts-about-the-attempted)
- **Time of flight is about 0.14 s.** Any head motion during that time matters for the model.

### Skill
- **Range practice.** Clairton Sportsmen's Club member since Aug 2023, with 43 logged sessions. About 80% of his time there was rifle practice, on ranges out to 200 yd. Last visit: July 12, 2024. [S] [Mon Valley Independent](https://www.monvalleyindependent.com/2024/08/09/records-show-trump-rally-gunman-practiced-dozens-of-times-at-clairton-sportsmens-club)
- **Online courses.** Basic carbine (May 2024) and handgun courses (June 2024). [S] [Judicial Watch / FBI records](https://www.judicialwatch.org/judicial-watch-new-fbi-records-reveal-details-about-thomas-crooks-behavior-before-trump-assassination-attempt/)
- **High-school rifle team.** He was rejected, partly as "a bad shot," per the team head. This was years earlier. [S] [TMZ/NY Post](https://www.tmz.com/2024/07/15/thomas-matthew-crooks-donald-trump-rejected-rifle-team-terrible-shot-attempted-assassination/)
- **He was rushed.**
  - The crowd was shouting about him from about 6:08.
  - An officer reached the roof edge at 6:10:55, and Crooks pointed the rifle at him.
  - He fired about 40 s later.
  - [S] Wikipedia; House TF
- **Expert views on difficulty.**
  - "Not a particularly difficult shot" (McBeth).
  - "Relatively easy" prone; clipping the ear "does not seem like terrible aim" (Myke Cole, [Slate](https://slate.com/news-and-politics/2024/07/trump-shooter-gun-thomas-crooks-ar-15-expertise-military.html)).
  - "Challenging with this setup" (Brandon Herrera).
- **Dispersion benchmarks.**
  - US Army: soldiers should "easily" print 4 MOA groups. [S] [Soldier Systems](https://soldiersystems.net/2016/12/30/82nd-abn-div-small-arms-master-gunner-on-new-25m-m16m4-zero-target/)
  - ARL-TR-7135 measured the radial error of 26 soldiers with M4s at 50/100/150 m. [S] [DTIC](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/ADA611755.pdf)
  - Corriveau et al. 2019: aiming error dominates for regular infantry. [S] [link](https://dpi-proceedings.com/index.php/ballistics31/article/view/33100)
- **Suggested starting inputs [I].**
  - Experienced and unhurried: per-axis SD about 0.6–1 MOA.
  - Novice or stressed: about 1.5–3 MOA.
  - Plus rifle and ammo dispersion, and an unknown zero bias.

## 6. Head and target geometry

- **Trump.** 6'3", 224 lb (April 2025 physical). Age 78 at the shooting. [S] [Fox](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/results-president-trumps-physical-released-what-they-say.amp)
- **ANSUR II male head size** (5th / 50th / 95th percentile):
  - Head length: 188 / 200 / 211 mm.
  - Head breadth: 146 / 154 / 163 mm.
  - [S] [NCSU tables](https://www.ergocenter.ncsu.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/18/2017/09/Anthropometric-Summary-Data-Tables.pdf)
  - Ear and neck columns are in the [ANSUR II CSV](https://calmcode.io/static/data/ergonomics.csv), not yet extracted.
- **Ear size and shape.**
  - Ears grow with age: length ≈ 55.9 + 0.22×age mm, so about 73 mm at 78. [S] [Heathcote BMJ 1995](https://jhanley.biostat.mcgill.ca/CommunicationCommunicationCommunication/bigears.pdf)
  - Protrusion from the head: about 11 mm at the top of the ear, 17 mm mid-ear, 21 mm at the lobe. [S] [Shah](https://www.shahfacialplastics.com/articles/anatomy-analysis-ear/)
- **Key geometric insight [I].**
  - Seen along the bullet's line, the ear lies almost entirely in front of the head's silhouette. The survivable "ear only" zone is just the rim where the ear sticks out past the skull's edge, about 0.4–0.7 in wide counting scalp and hair.
  - That is roughly 1–1.5 in² against about 60–70 in² of lethal head and neck, **about 2%**.
  - A rough 1-D sketch (aim at head center, 150 yd) gives about a 3–4% chance per shot of landing in the ear band on the correct side, at almost any skill level.
  - **This ignores the turn angle, aim point, and the ear's height. It is only an illustration.**
- **Anatomy on the counterfactual paths [I].**
  - A few mm inward: the temporoparietal skull and brain.
  - A "half-turn": the parietal or occipital lobes.
  - Lower: the cerebellum or brainstem; through the neck, the carotid or jugular vessels and the spine.
  - At about 2,600 fps, all of these are almost certainly lethal or devastating.

## 8. Where sources disagree

1. **Distance.** Figures run from 400 to 465 ft. The likely cause is different reference points. The DHS OIG gives 155 yd and Maher's acoustics give 130 m.
2. **Sights.** Early reports said "iron sights." The FBI evidence list shows a 1× red dot.
3. **Shot grouping.** "1 + 2 + 5" (Wikipedia) versus "3 + 5" (NYT, Higgins).
4. **Timing of the local officer's shot.** About 4 s (Wikipedia), about 5.5 s (CBS), or 5.89 s (Higgins).
5. **What stopped Crooks.** Higgins says the local officer's round hit the stock. The FBI says there is no definitive forensic proof of that.
6. **Second shooter.** Grigoras's acoustic claim of a second weapon is contradicted by Maher, the 8 casings, and the FBI lab. Treat it as unsupported.
7. **The Mills streak.** It may show round 1 or round 2.
8. **How close the bullet came.** 1/8 in (Trump), < 1/4 in (Jackson), "an inch" (Slate). None of these was measured.
9. **Fatal counter-sniper distance.** About 180–200 yd, not the "~100 yd" sometimes quoted. The 100 yd figure belongs to the local officer.

## 9. Unknowns that matter for the model (ranked)

1. **Where shots 2–8 went in Trump's frame of reference**, especially shots 2–3. The House TF Quantico pages are the best unread lead.
2. **Trump's exact head pose at shot 1.** We have the turn angle only from video, and no published measurement.
3. **Crooks's aim point.** Head or center mass?
4. **Exact firing position and stage/podium coordinates and heights.** PA LiDAR and dated imagery can fix this.
5. **Ammunition type**, and whether the dot sight was zeroed.
6. **Bleacher layout and victim positions.**
