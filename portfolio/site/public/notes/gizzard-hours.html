How this was counted

  • Watch history records that something played, never for how long. Minutes come from MusicBrainz track durations, assuming each play finished — so a skipped track counts the same as a full one.
  • Music counts, talk does not: concert footage, bootlegs and drum-cams are listening; 14 plays of interviews, podcasts, reactions and gear rundowns were excluded.
  • A live version counts toward the album the song comes from, so a bootleg Robot Stop lands on Nonagon Infinity.
  • 52 plays named no track with a known runtime — mostly full-set uploads and medleys — and contribute nothing rather than a guess.
