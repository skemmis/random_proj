Shell Game

Every home on this map whose real owner is kept off the public record. Walk to one to claim it. The harder it is to say who owns it, the more it's worth.

How hidden

  • Common1–4 pts
  • Uncommon5–7 pts
  • Rare8–11 pts
  • Legendary12+ pts
  1. Owned by a company or a non-family trust +1
  2. Nobody claims it as their home +1
  3. Tax bills go to a registered-agent office +3, a PO box or a lawyer +1, or out of state +1
  4. 5 or more other homes share that mailing address +2, 25 or more +3
  5. Formed in Delaware, Wyoming, Nevada, New Mexico or South Dakota +2, offshore +4
  6. Each layer of companies before the trail goes cold +1, up to +4
  7. A name in the chain is in the ICIJ Offshore Leaks +5 or on a sanctions list +6
  8. A small local landlord tops out at Common, whatever state the LLC is from
