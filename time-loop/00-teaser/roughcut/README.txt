TEASER KIT: the rough cut as a DaVinci Resolve timeline

Open it
1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. In Resolve, make a new project. In Project Settings, set the timeline to 1920 x 1080 at 30 fps.
3. Drag the whole teaser-kit folder into the Media Pool.
4. File > Import > Timeline, and choose teaser-roughcut.xml. Untick "Automatically import source clips into media pool", so Resolve uses the clips you just added.
5. If any clip shows red (offline), right-click it and choose Relink, then point to this folder.

What's on the timeline
- V1 holds the main shots in order. Each loop opens on the clock turning to 6:00 and Phil getting out of bed: pass 1 (long takes), pass 2 (tighter, with the motion graphics) and pass 3 (manic). Then a second of black, the fist raise, and a 3-second freeze.
- V2 to V5 hold the four-way split near the end of pass 3.
- A1 is each shot's own sound. A2 is sound carried over a cut into the next shot. A3 is the Butler screams rising under pass 3. A4 is "I Got You Babe" under the whole teaser, in three pieces: full over the clock, ducked 16 dB, full again for the fist. It is the film's own clock-radio song stitched into one take (song-bed.mp4); drop a clean MP3 on A4 to replace it. A5 to A7 carry the sound of the four-way split.
- Every clip has spare footage at both ends, so you can drag any edge longer.
- The motion graphics are the piece's own renders, each with a one-second hold at both ends. Some are in the folder but not on the timeline yet, for you to place: wake-3, wake-5 and alarm-late are spare wake-ups; chart-scrub and the word clips are left over from the first cut.
