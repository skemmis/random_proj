# The Loop Hypothesis

Sam started this spin-off of the Butler project on October 13, 2026. It is a straight-faced, half-satirical investigation of the claim that Donald Trump is trapped in a time loop like the one in *Groundhog Day*, told as a series of New York Times-style interactive pages, one idea per page. The "Time loop" thread owns this folder. The plan, with every film, trait and clip, is in `PLAN.md`.

Piece 1 notes. Version 2 (October 13, 22:45, after Sam asked for the videos to be the star) is a full-screen, scroll-snapping film: an opening clock, six scenes laid out side by side or as a draggable wipe, a finale where the reader relives July 13 over the full-screen Butler footage until the ear-only day comes, and a credits roll. Sam writes the copy; all text and video slots are in the CONTENT block of `template.html`, and `CLIPS.md` lists the slots. Version 1, the scrolling article, is kept as `template_v1.html`. `template.html` holds the page; `frames.json` holds four YouTube preview frames per clip (thumbnail plus YouTube's automatic frames at 25, 50 and 75 percent), cropped to 16:9 and stored as data URIs; `build.py` writes `index.html`. The page cannot embed video, so each clip is a looping four-frame strip with a link to YouTube. The Butler figures (1 in 990 ear only, 1 in 105 graze, updated October 14 after the camera fix) are quoted from `butler-shooting/results/RESULTS.md` and `results/speech_luck.json`; the page computes only 1 − (1 − 1/990)^N.
