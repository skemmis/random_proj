# The Loop Hypothesis: one thing on screen

This is a proposal; nothing is published yet. It follows Sam's rule (October 15, 23:13) and design/LOOK.md. The mock is `exhibit2-before.png` and `exhibit2-after.png`: laptop on the left, phone on the right.

## What every exhibit does

1. **The title, alone.** The exhibit's title ("No consequences") sits on a still frame of the film for about three seconds before anything plays. It then fades out and does not come back over the video.
2. **The film side, full screen.** It plays across the whole screen, with one line on it: the quote, plus a small attribution (for example, "Phil Connors, Groundhog Day").
3. **The Trump side, full screen.** It plays the same way, with its own quote.
4. **One comparison beat.** At each switch, the two sides sit side by side for under a second, then the next side takes the screen. That beat is the only time two things are on screen.

## What goes

- The 6:00 clock and the day counter in the corner. In the video the clock now marks each loop, so the page doesn't need it.
- The dots down the right edge.
- On each clip, the tag, the "who" line and the credit line. They move to the sources panel; the attribution keeps the film and the name.
- The progress bar.
- The pause and sound buttons inside the joined page, which already owns sound. The standalone page keeps them in its corner.
- The dimmed second side while the first plays.
