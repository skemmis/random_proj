# The Loop Hypothesis: clip notes for the sources panel

Five exhibits since v24; order and exhibit 3 as of v25 (Oct 16). The page shows only a quote and a name line on each clip, so these lines are for assembly to fold into `assembly/sources-panel/60-loop.md`. The only network named here that isn't C-SPAN, CNN or MSNBC is the Butler broadcast, which the panel already credits.

- Exhibit 1. "Groundhog Day" (Columbia Pictures, 1993): Phil, drunk at the wheel, asks two locals what they would do if there were no tomorrow. Trump at Dordt College, Sioux Center, Iowa, Jan. 23, 2016 (CNN, via the Internet Archive TV News Archive).
- Exhibit 2. "Edge of Tomorrow" (Warner Bros., 2014): Cage (Tom Cruise) is shot in training and tries again. Trump at the Republican National Convention, Milwaukee, July 18, 2024: "If I had not moved my head at that very last instant..." (C-SPAN, via the Internet Archive TV News Archive).
- Exhibit 3. "Palm Springs" (Hulu and Neon, 2020): Nyles (Andy Samberg) drinks from other wedding guests' glasses. Trump at the NATO summit, Brussels, May 25, 2017, shoving past Duško Marković, Montenegro's prime minister (MSNBC, via the Internet Archive TV News Archive).
- Exhibit 4. "Groundhog Day": Phil gives the same forecast again. Trump's town hall in Oaks, Pa., Oct. 14, 2024, where he stopped taking questions and swayed to music (C-SPAN, via the Internet Archive TV News Archive).
- Exhibit 5. "Palm Springs": Nyles takes an arrow and lies back on his float. Butler, Pa., July 13, 2024, a minute after the shot: Trump rises and raises his fist (the Butler broadcast, via the Internet Archive).
